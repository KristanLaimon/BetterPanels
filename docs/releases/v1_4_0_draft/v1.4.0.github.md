# Panels+ | The Formats & Precision Update 

Built from tag: `v1.4.0`

This is the Formats & Precision update! v1.4.0 brings some of the biggest improvements to Panels+ yet: full support for reading embedded comic and manga images inside reflowable books (**EPUB**, **KEPUB**, and **MOBI**) with cross-page navigation flow, a brand new **Connected-Components Panel Detector** that shatters our previous accuracy records with **>95% precision and recall**, new **hardware-accelerated page and panel slide animations**, **per-document saved settings**, inverted tap and swipe controls, restored and expanded **Kobo physical buttons and remote support**, **keyboard shortcuts**, and our first hand-crafted **Spanish translation**.

Here are all the new features and improvements in detail:

## A Note on Panel Detection &amp; The New Dataset Benchmarks

Following user feedback on difficult-to-detect pages, I invested significant effort into building an automated benchmarking framework with 4 complete manga and comic volumes (over 760 pages and 2,890 real panels: *Bloom Into You Vol. 8*, *Komi Can't Communicate Vol. 1*, *Miss Kobayashi's Dragon Maid Vol. 2*, and *Scott Pilgrim Vol. 5*):

- **Connected Components over X-Y Cuts:** The new `components` detector groups 8-connected ink pixels and fits bounding frames. It effortlessly handles angled panel borders, speech bubbles breaking panel gutters, and colored backgrounds without needing straight page-wide cuts.
- **Accuracy Jump:** Overall detection precision rose from **78.2% to 95.8%**, recall from **80.5% to 93.8%**, and pages with 100% perfect reading order jumped from **46.9% to 81.9%**.
- **Low-RAM Friendly:** To keep this snappy on Kindle and Kobo e-ink devices, the detector uses reusable pre-allocated FFI scratch buffers with `memset` zeroing, avoiding Lua garbage-collection overhead during page turns.

## A Note on Navigation Modes (Classic, Smooth, Animated)

- **Classic:** The fastest and most battery-efficient mode. Instantly switches between panels with zero animation delay — recommended for low-spec e-ink devices.
- **Smooth:** Pans the camera across the page from panel to panel. Configurable frame rate (down to 1 fps for e-ink partial refresh, up to 24 fps for smooth displays).
- **Animated:** Uses KOReader's framebuffer hardware slide effect to animate panel switches and page turns. Because e-ink displays take time to flash and refresh, Animated mode is best suited for non-e-ink hardware (like Android tablets, phones, or PCs), though it is fully available on all devices if you enjoy the transition.

## Fixes

- Fixed reading flow sequencing where panels in complex or irregular Western comic layouts were visited out of narrative order.
- Fixed stacked vertical panels in Manga mode causing erratic jumps instead of following top-to-bottom reading order within columns.
- Fixed device orientation being reset or overwritten by KOReader's global document configuration when changing orientation inside Panels+.
- Fixed panel zoom level not being restored properly when rotating the screen while reading embedded images in reflowable books.
- Fixed reversed swipe directions in Manga and Comic modes so that swipe gestures naturally align with reading flow (with an option to invert if preferred).
- Fixed "Smooth" navigation ignoring the "With margin" crop setting and losing panel margin during camera pans.
- Fixed smooth navigation options not being available for embedded images in reflowable formats (.epub, .mobi, .kepub).
- Fixed physical page-turn button navigation regressions on Kobo hardware and Bluetooth page-turner devices.
- Fixed sudden viewer close / crashes when a document was closed or modified by external plugins by adding safe-guards.
- Shortened and cleaned up verbose menu labels (e.g. `[Text Selection]: Touch & hold`).
- Fixed potential memory leaks and redundant page rasterizations when navigating across consecutive embedded images.
- Reduced CPU overhead and added reusable scratch buffers to keep panel detection snappy on low-RAM e-ink readers.

## Upgrading

Copy `panels_plus.koplugin` over your existing folder and restart KOReader. Your settings and reading progress are kept. All new navigation options (including Nav. Animated and inverted tap/swipe controls) can be configured directly from the panel viewer's "More config..." menu.
